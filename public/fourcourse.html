GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might be wondering.

Is it free to join?

Creating a FourCourse account is free. Any expected meal or reservation costs will be explained before you confirm a dinner invitation.

Does choosing a date mean I’m committed?

No. Your selected dates tell us when you may be available. You’ll have a chance to review and confirm an actual dinner invitation before making a commitment.

Can I change my availability later?

Yes. Sign in to your dashboard to add or remove dates and keep your schedule up to date.

When will I hear about a dinner?

Dinner invitations depend on organizer-led group planning and restaurant arrangements. We’ll share the details once a dinner is confirmed; submitting availability alone is not a reservation.

Will I have a public profile?

No. FourCourse is not a public profile or swiping app. Your account and preferences are used to help organize the dinner experience.
