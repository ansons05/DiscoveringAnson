How FourCourse Works

Here’s what happens before, during, and after your dinner. All FourCourse dinners take place from 7:00 to 9:00 PM.

01

Sign up and tell us about yourself

Choose your school, answer 20 short compatibility questions, and share the days you’re available for dinner. The questions help us understand your personality and preferences—there are no right or wrong answers.

02

Share your dinner preferences

Tell us your seating preference, note any allergies, dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or other considerations you want us to know about, and provide your school email so we can contact you.

03

We organize your dinner group

FourCourse uses your responses and availability to arrange a dinner group with students from your school. Once the group is organized, we’ll email you the dinner plan and details.

04

Meet your group—and follow up

Attend the dinner, meet your fellow students, and enjoy the conversation. Afterward, you’ll be asked to complete a short follow-up survey and send FourCourse a group photo. FourCourse reserves the right to use submitted photos in promotional materials.

Before submitting, you’ll review and agree to the participation commitments, including the two-day cancellation policy, follow-up survey, and group photo.
